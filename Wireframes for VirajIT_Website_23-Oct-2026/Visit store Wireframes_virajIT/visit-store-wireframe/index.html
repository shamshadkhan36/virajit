A Few Things Worth Knowing.

Planning to visit for a refurbished laptop, brand-new machine, printer, or repair? A quick enquiry can help you prepare.

Can I check laptop availability before visiting?

Yes. Contact our team on WhatsApp at +91 89287 15729 to check whether specific laptop models or configurations are currently in stock.

Can I see and test refurbished laptops in person?

Yes! Every customer is encouraged to visit our store, inspect the physical condition, test the display, keyboard, battery health, and run benchmarks before making a purchase.

Can I bring my laptop directly for repair?

Yes. You can walk in during our store hours (Mon–Sat 10 AM–8:30 PM, Sun 11 AM–6 PM). Our engineers will perform an immediate diagnostic check.

What is the best landmark to locate the store?

We are situated at Shop No. 31, Ground Floor, Shreeji Darshan Building, on Shantilal Modi Road, right near the Bata Showroom in Kandivali West (within walking distance of Kandivali Railway Station).

Are credit card and debit card EMIs accepted?

Yes, all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and EMI options are supported in-store.
