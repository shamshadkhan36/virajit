# Viraj IT — revised homepage wireframe

Open `index.html` in a browser. This local wireframe applies the requested homepage hierarchy and uses the shared visual styles from `../laptop-brands-wireframe/styles.css`. Keep both folders together when sharing the files.

## Visitor journey

Hero: refurbished laptops and repair → three offerings → budget entry points and live product module → repair → paid rental → new brands → store trust and genuine reviews → visit/contact → FAQ.

## Reuse when integrating into the production homepage

- Keep the existing header, footer, floating WhatsApp component, routes, SEO metadata and analytics. The wireframe header/footer show desired navigation and content only.
- Mount the existing dynamic product component in the indicated product area. Select 4–6 currently available items and preserve its live prices, stock, enquiry actions, and loading/empty/error states. The grey cards are structural placeholders, not products.
- Reuse original review text and photographs in the combined customer-proof section. The current review count/rating should be pulled from a verified source, not copied from this wireframe.
- Remove the separate premium product section and the Videos & Shorts block from the homepage. Keep those routes and videos on appropriate supporting pages.
- The budget links use the live site's current working routes, whose slugs do not match their displayed thresholds. Validate the 25–40k and over-40k filters when integrating.
- The Laptop Brands links point to the companion local wireframe. Replace them with `/laptop-brands/` after that route is implemented.

## Verify before publication

- Approved product, technician, customer and storefront photography; approved brand logos and partnership wording.
- Full address, opening hours, WhatsApp/phone details, direct Google reviews URL, and Google Maps directions URL. The address and directions link in this wireframe are carried over from the current live site and should be checked.
- Warranty and EMI terms for each product. The FAQ does not promise universal terms.
- Support contact details in the existing footer; the live footer currently shows a different repair-support number that should be confirmed.

No live site or production repository was changed. This is a reviewable design source for integration once the current site source is available.
