04 / DOCUMENTED REPAIR WORK

A Closer Look at Our Work

Real repair examples demonstrating the technical problems our team solves and how the devices are restored.

CASE STUDY 01

Overheating & Clogged Fan Servicing

An HP Pavilion gaming laptop was shutting down automatically within 15 minutes of use due to 98°C thermal throttling. We disassembled the cooling module, removed dense lint blockages, replaced degraded thermal paste with high-conductivity compound, and lubricated the blower fan.

Result: Temperatures dropped to steady 44°C idle / 72°C peak load.
CASE STUDY 02

Screen & Body Hinge Fabrication

A Dell Inspiron had broken plastic screw posts in the bottom chassis, causing the screen bezel to pop open and display cables to pinch whenever the lid moved. Our technician reconstructed the anchor mounts using structural resin fabrication and installed a new IPS FHD screen.

Result: Smooth hinge tension restored with perfect panel alignment.
CASE STUDY 03

SSD & RAM Speed Transformation

A customer brought an 8th-Gen Lenovo laptop with a slow 1TB 5400RPM mechanical hard drive taking over 4 minutes to boot. We cloned the existing operating system and all files onto a 512GB High-Speed NVMe SSD, expanded the RAM to 16GB dual-channel, with 100% data intact.

Result: Boot time reduced from 4 minutes to 9 seconds flat.

07 / GOOD TO KNOW

Repair FAQs

Do you repair laptops from different brands?

Yes. We service all brands including Dell, HP, Lenovo ThinkPad/IdeaPad, Asus ROG/ZenBook, Acer Aspire/Nitro, MSI, and Apple MacBook. We stock genuine screens, batteries, keyboards, and internal chips.

Can I get my laptop checked before deciding on a repair?

Yes! Our technicians perform an initial diagnostic check to identify the exact cause and give you an upfront price quotation. Work only begins after you approve the estimate.

Do you perform motherboard chip-level repair?

Yes. Viraj IT specializes in micro-soldering, short-circuit troubleshooting on power rails, charging IC replacement, and BGA chip repairs, saving you the high cost of whole motherboard replacement.

How long does a laptop repair take?

Routine repairs like screen replacement, SSD/RAM upgrades, battery replacement, and thermal fan cleaning are completed on the same day (usually in 1–3 hours). Complex motherboard chip repairs typically take 24–48 hours.

Is there a warranty on laptop repairs?

Yes! All hardware repairs and replacement components carry a warranty period covering the work performed. Full details are printed on your invoice.

Will my personal data remain safe during repair?

Yes. We respect complete client confidentiality and privacy. For hardware repairs (such as screen, hinge, or fan replacement), your storage drive does not need to be formatted.
