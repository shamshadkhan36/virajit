07 / GOOD TO KNOW

Repair FAQs

Do you repair laptops from different brands?

Yes. Tell our team the brand, model and issue. We repair all major brands including Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus, Acer, Apple MacBook, and Toshiba.

Can I get my laptop checked before deciding on a repair?

You can request an assessment and review the recommended work and estimated costs before approving a repair. Ask the team about diagnostic charges.

Do you repair laptop motherboards?

Viraj IT specializes in chip-level motherboard repair, power IC replacement, BGA rework, and liquid damage restoration after detailed diagnosis.

How long does a laptop repair take?

Routine repairs like screen, battery, fan servicing, or SSD upgrade are completed the same day. Complex chip-level motherboard repairs usually take 24–48 hours.

Is there a warranty on laptop repairs?

Yes. All hardware repairs and replacement parts come with a store replacement warranty. Confirm specific warranty terms for your repair with the technician.

Should I back up my data before repair?

If you can access your files, make a backup before handing over the laptop. If your laptop doesn't power on, our technician can perform a safe data backup upon request.
